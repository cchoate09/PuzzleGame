Welcome to Patchwork Post

            
You're a postkeeper restoring delivery routes through a paper-craft town torn apart by a storm. Each room is a puzzle to solve.

          

          

            
📥

            
Your Goal

            
Reach the golden mailbox to restore each route. Look for the glowing gold tile with a small brown flag on top.

            

              
              This is the mailbox — your destination.
            

          

          

            
🎮

            
Moving Around

            
Use Arrow keys or WASD to move one tile at a time. Brown blocks are walls you can't pass through.

            

              
              You — the blue circle with a tan face.
            

            

              
              Walls — impassable.
            

          

          

            
🧵

            
Layers & Stitches

            
Rooms are made of stacked paper sheets. The active layer has a gold border. Stand on a stitch marker and press Tab to flip between layers.

            

              
              Stitch marker — your portal between layers.
            

          

          

            
📦

            
Parcels & Transfer

            
Brown boxes are parcels you can push by walking into them. Face a parcel and press X to transfer it to the same spot on another layer.

            

              
              Parcel — push or transfer between layers.
            

          

          

            
💡

            
Helpful Tools

            
Press Z to undo a move, R to reset the room, and click Hint if you get stuck. There's no penalty for experimenting!
